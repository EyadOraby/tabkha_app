# Brand

**Tabkha** (طبخة, "a dish cooked at home"). Warm, home-kitchen food ordering.

## Personality

A neighbour's kitchen, not a delivery app: warm, generous and a little handmade. Friendly, never loud.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Herb green | `#3F6B3A` | Primary actions, logo, selected states |
| Tomato | `#D9583B` | Accents, location pin, warnings |
| Saffron | `#E3A33A` | Ratings, placed orders |
| Crust | `#B9773D` | Accepted orders, warm details |
| Cream | `#FFF8EC` | Page background |
| Paper | `#FFFDF8` | Cards and fields, with `#EADCC4` borders |
| Ink | `#2B2118` | Text (a warm brown, never pure black) |

## Type

- **Display:** Young Serif, with Lalezar for Arabic
- **Text:** Nunito, with Baloo Bhaijaan 2 for Arabic
- All four are SIL Open Font License, bundled in `assets/fonts/brand`.

## Shapes

Generous rounding: stadium buttons and chips, 18 px cards with thin cream borders, no drop shadows and no gradients except dark scrims on food photos.

## Illustration

Hand-drawn ingredient doodles (`lib/Features/Food/Widgets/kitchen_art.dart`): tomato, garlic, chili, basil, lemon, onion, wooden spoon, pot and bread. Each has a flat colour shape printed slightly off its ink outline, like a two-colour risograph. They appear beside the Discover headline, around the logo on sign-in and in every empty state.

## Avoid

Cold blues and purples, emoji as icons, glossy gradients and generic "food app" copy.
