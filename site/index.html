Order dinner, or cook it yourself.

Tabkha brings restaurant delivery and home recipes together in one app. Restaurants run their kitchen from a dashboard, and the platform team approves restaurants and watches every order.

Open the live demo → Restaurant dashboard ⬇ Android app

Three apps in one

Customers, restaurant owners and platform admins each get their own side of Tabkha, all sharing the same live orders.

🛵

Delivery

Search restaurants and dishes, filter by cuisine, build a basket and follow your order from placed to delivered.

📖

Recipes

Hundreds of recipes with ingredients, numbered steps and favourites saved on your device.

⭐

Reviews

Rate delivered orders; restaurant ratings update for everyone.

🍳

Restaurant dashboard

Accept orders, move them through the kitchen, mark dishes sold out and add new ones.

🛡️

Platform admin

Approve or suspend restaurants, see gross sales and a table of every order.

🌍

Arabic and English

Switch language live with full right-to-left layouts. Sign in with Firebase or continue as a guest.

Dashboards for restaurants and admins

On a wide screen the dashboards use a side rail; on a phone they switch to bottom tabs. Try the platform view at ?screen=superadmin.

How it's built

Riverpod holds the state, Hive keeps it on the device, recipes come from TheMealDB, and Firebase handles sign-in. Tests and CI run on every push, and every merge deploys this site.
